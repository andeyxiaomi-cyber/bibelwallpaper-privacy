Privacy Policy for BibelWallpaper

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Who we are

BibelWallpaper is an Android app that sets Bible verses as wallpaper on the lock screen and/or home screen of your device.

Data collection

BibelWallpaper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app:

  • has no internet connection (the app does not hold network access permission);
  • uses no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs;
  • requires no account or sign-in;
  • never sends any information to third-party servers, because no such servers exist for this app.

Photo access

If you choose a background image from your gallery, the app uses Android's built-in Photo Picker. Through it, the app gets access only to the single image you selected - not to your entire gallery. The selected image is used solely to display on your wallpaper and never leaves your phone.

Stored settings

All app settings (language, font, colors, selected verse, etc.) are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data along with it.

Home screen widget

The widget displays a verse from the app's built-in collection and works on the same principles - no data ever leaves your device.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, an updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
